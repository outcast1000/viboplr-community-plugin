## 0.1.0

- First release. A **Community** view where you can browse and search cue sheets on Viboplr Community, find the ones for the song that's playing, and import one with a single click.
- **Sign in with GitHub** to publish your own sheets from **My sheets**, update them, or unpublish them. Browsing and importing need no account.
- Share links (`viboplr://plugin/community/open?id=…`) open the sheet in the app, ready to import.
- Right-click a track and choose **Find shared cue sheets** or **Publish cue sheet…**.
- A **Servers** tab with the Subsonic / Navidrome servers their owners opened to everyone (formerly servers.viboplr.com). **Add** opens the app's own Add Server dialog, already filled in; you confirm there. **List a server** opens the form on the website.
